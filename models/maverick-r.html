Engine & Transmission

Every Maverick R shares the same heart: Can-Am's Rotax 999T, a 999cc turbocharged triple-cylinder engine with an integrated intercooler and a high-performance air filter, producing 240 hp — among the highest factory output in the sport SxS class. Instead of a CVT belt drive, the Maverick R uses a genuine 7-speed dual-clutch transmission (DCT) with High and Extra Low range, giving it more direct, connected power delivery than a typical belt-driven competitor.

Suspension & Chassis

The Maverick R runs a forged-aluminum, heavy-duty tall-knuckle suspension at all four corners — double A-arm with sway bar up front, a 4-link trailing arm setup in the rear — with up to 25 inches of travel depending on trim and configuration. Base and X trims run FOX QS3 shocks, while higher trims offer Smart-Shox™, Can-Am's electronically adjustable damping system.

Maverick R Trim Lineup

Same 240-hp platform across every trim — the differences come down to suspension tuning, tires, seating, and terrain focus.

Maverick R

The base two-seat platform with the full 240-hp DCT drivetrain and no race-specific add-ons — a strong starting point for owners who plan to build out suspension, wheels, or armor over time.

Maverick R X

Adds factory-installed upgrades over the base R for improved durability and trail-readiness out of the gate, without stepping up to full race-level pricing.

Maverick R X rc

Can-Am's rock-crawling package — same 999T engine, but with an extra-low first gear, a Rock drive mode, larger 35-in. tires, and a factory JL Audio system for slower, technical terrain.

Maverick R MAX

The four-seat version of the platform on a stretched wheelbase, keeping the same 240-hp engine, Smart-Lok differential, and suspension architecture as the two-seat R.
