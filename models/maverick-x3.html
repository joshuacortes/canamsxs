Engine & Transmission

The Maverick X3 is powered by Can-Am's Rotax ACE 900, a 900cc turbocharged triple-cylinder engine with an integrated intercooler and high-performance air filter. Output ranges from 135 hp on entry-level Turbo trims up to 200 hp on Turbo RR models. Rather than a dual-clutch gearbox, the X3 uses Can-Am's pDrive primary clutch paired with the QRS-X CVT, tuned for high airflow and reduced belt wear across a wide power band.

Suspension & Chassis

The Maverick X3 runs an arched double A-arm front suspension with sway bar and a trailing arm rear, on a lightweight, race-proven chassis. Travel starts at 20 inches on base Turbo trims and stretches to 24 inches on top-spec RR models. Shock hardware scales with trim, from SHOWA HPG piggyback units on entry trims up through FOX 2.5 Podium RC2 and FOX 3.0 Podium shocks, with Smart-Shox™ electronically adjustable damping available on select packages.

Maverick X3 Trim Lineup

One turbocharged platform, tuned across a wide trim range for dunes, trails, mud, and everything between — the differences come down to horsepower, shocks, tires, and width.

Maverick X3 Turbo

The entry point to the X3 lineup, with the 135-hp Rotax ACE turbo triple, SHOWA HPG shocks, and 20 in. of travel — a strong trail-focused starting platform.

Maverick X3 X

Steps up shock hardware and trail-readiness over the base Turbo, with FOX 2.5 Podium RC2 piggyback shocks and factory tech like a touchscreen infotainment system.

Maverick X3 RS

The dune- and desert-oriented package, built around a wider 72-in. stance for high-speed stability across open terrain.

Maverick X3 X RR / RS Turbo RR

The top of the lineup, pairing the full 200-hp Turbo RR engine with premium FOX shocks and beadlock-capable wheels for riders who want maximum factory power.
