Can-Am SxS Electronics & Audio Upgrades

GPS and comms upgrades, Bluetooth stereo systems, and speaker/amp builds for Maverick R, Maverick X3, Defender & Commander — jump to your model below.

Maverick R Electronics Maverick X3 Electronics Defender Electronics Commander Electronics

Maverick R Electronics & Audio Upgrades

Every Maverick R trim comes with a 10.25-in. touchscreen display running Can-Am's factory infotainment system, so most electronics upgrades here build on top of that rather than replacing it — adding a dedicated audio system, mounting a GPS/comms unit for group rides, or wiring in auxiliary switches for lighting and accessories.

OEM Upgrade Path

Can-Am's accessory catalog offers a factory-fit Bluetooth audio system designed around the Maverick R's dash and roll cage mounting points — worth checking with your dealer first if you want factory-matched fitment and weatherproofing.

Aftermarket Options

  • Rockford Fosgate — Stage Series Audio Kits: Roll-cage-mount speaker and amp kits built for high-speed desert use without losing clarity over wind and engine noise.
  • Rugged Radios — Bluetooth Intercom Systems: A common add-on for group desert running, letting riders communicate without a hardwired harness.
  • PCI Race Radios — Elite Series Intercom: Race-proven intercom hardware for owners running chase or multi-vehicle convoys.
  • Garmin — Overlander / Tread GPS: Off-road-specific GPS units that mount cleanly to the Maverick R's dash for trail mapping and tracking.

Maverick X3 Electronics & Audio Upgrades

X3 trims vary widely on factory infotainment, so upgrades here range from adding a display where none exists to building out a full audio system for owners running the Max four-seat platform. Intercoms and GPS units are also common, especially for owners doing longer desert or dune trips in groups.

OEM Upgrade Path

Can-Am's accessory catalog includes a factory stereo/speaker kit sized for both two-seat and Max four-seat X3 cabs — a good starting point if you want a factory-matched look before adding aftermarket components.

Aftermarket Options

  • MTX Audio — RZR/X3 Speaker Pods & Amp Kits: A popular roll-cage-mount speaker system for X3 owners building out a full audio setup.
  • Rugged Radios — Bluetooth Intercom Systems: Widely used for X3 group rides and dune trips where hand signals aren't practical.
  • Rigid Industries — Switch-Pro Control Systems: A common add-on for wiring auxiliary lighting and accessories through a single sealed switch panel.

Defender Electronics & Audio Upgrades

Defender's utility role means electronics upgrades here lean practical — backup cameras for trailer work, auxiliary switch panels for worksite lighting, and GPS or two-way radio setups for owners covering large properties or ranch land, alongside audio for owners who want music on longer trail rides.

OEM Upgrade Path

Ask your dealer about Can-Am's factory audio and auxiliary switch accessory packages for Defender — they're built to integrate with the factory wiring harness without voiding warranty coverage.

Aftermarket Options

  • Rockford Fosgate — General/Defender Audio Kits: A direct-fit speaker and amp system sized for Defender's dash and cage layout.
  • Rugged Radios — Two-Way Radio Kits: Common for ranch, farm, and large-property use where cell coverage is unreliable.
  • Rigid Industries — Switch-Pro Control Systems: A sealed auxiliary switch panel for wiring up worklights and accessories cleanly.

Commander Electronics & Audio Upgrades

Commander owners tend to split between trail-focused audio setups and the same practical GPS/comms upgrades seen on Defender, since the platform gets used for both recreational riding and light utility work. A Bluetooth stereo system is the most common single add-on across this platform.

OEM Upgrade Path

Check with your dealer on Can-Am's factory Bluetooth audio accessory kit for Commander before going aftermarket — it's built to mount cleanly to the factory dash and roll cage.

Aftermarket Options

  • MTX Audio — Commander Speaker Pods & Amp Kits: A popular roll-cage-mount audio setup for Commander owners.
  • Rugged Radios — Bluetooth Intercom Systems: Used for both group trail rides and basic worksite communication.
  • Garmin — Tread GPS: An off-road GPS unit for owners covering unmarked trail or large properties.
