Maverick R Lighting Upgrades

Maverick R's forged tall-knuckle A-arms and 77-in. width give it a wide, high front end that suits a full-width light bar without much fabrication. Owners running the platform at speed in the desert typically prioritize long-throw forward lighting, while rock/chase lights on the rear bumper help trailing vehicles keep a visual line in dust.

OEM Upgrade Path

Can-Am's accessory catalog includes a factory-fit LED light bar and pod kit sized to the Maverick R's front bumper and roll cage mounting points — worth checking with your dealer first for plug-and-play wiring that ties into the factory harness.

Aftermarket Options

  • Rigid Industries — Adapt XE Light Bar: A high-output bar with selectable beam patterns, popular for desert running where light needs change with terrain and speed.
  • Baja Designs — OnX6 Light Bar: A widely used long-throw light bar for high-speed forward visibility.
  • KC HiLiTES — Gravity Pro6 Pods: Compact ditch-light-style pods that mount to the A-pillar for wide-angle side lighting in turns.
  • Rigid Industries — Chase Light Kits: Amber rear lighting for chase and convoy visibility in dust or low light.

Maverick X3 Lighting Upgrades

X3's front bumper and roll cage are common mounting points for a full light bar setup, and owners running dunes or desert at night typically pair a forward light bar with A-pillar ditch lights for better peripheral visibility in whoops and turns.

OEM Upgrade Path

Can-Am's accessory catalog offers a factory LED light bar kit for X3 that mounts to the stock front bumper — a good starting point if you want factory-matched fitment before adding aftermarket lighting.

Aftermarket Options

  • Baja Designs — OnX6 Light Bar: A common upgrade for X3 owners wanting long-throw forward lighting for high-speed desert running.
  • Rigid Industries — Radiance Pod Kits: Multi-color accent pods often mounted to the cage for both function and appearance.
  • KC HiLiTES — Gravity Pro6 Ditch Lights: A-pillar-mounted pods for wide-angle side visibility.
  • Vision X — Rock Crawler Lights: Compact underbody or bumper-mounted lights for illuminating tight trail sections.

Defender Lighting Upgrades

Defender's utility role puts a premium on wide, even worksite-style lighting rather than long-throw desert beams — owners commonly add a roof-mounted light bar for illuminating a wide work area, plus rear worklights for loading and hitching after dark.

OEM Upgrade Path

Ask your dealer about Can-Am's factory roof-mount LED light bar and rear worklight accessory kits for Defender — both are designed to wire into the factory harness without additional splicing.

Aftermarket Options

  • Rigid Industries — Adapt XE Light Bar: A flood-focused beam pattern well suited to wide worksite illumination.
  • Baja Designs — S8 Light Bar: A durable roof or bumper-mount bar built for regular utility use.
  • Vision X — Rear Utility Worklights: Compact rear-mounted lights for hitching, loading, and backing up trailers after dark.

Commander Lighting Upgrades

Commander owners typically split between trail-focused forward lighting for night rides and the same practical worksite lighting seen on Defender, since the platform sees both recreational and light utility use. A front light bar paired with rear worklights covers most of what owners add here.

OEM Upgrade Path

Check with your dealer on Can-Am's factory LED light bar accessory kit for Commander before going aftermarket — it mounts directly to the factory front bumper and wires into the existing harness.

Aftermarket Options

  • Baja Designs — OnX6 Light Bar: A popular forward-lighting upgrade for Commander owners doing night trail riding.
  • Rigid Industries — Radiance Pod Kits: Cage-mounted accent pods for added visibility and styling.
  • Vision X — Rear Utility Worklights: Rear-mounted lighting for hitching and loading in low light.
