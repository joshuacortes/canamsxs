Maverick R Exhaust & Performance Upgrades

The Maverick R's turbocharged 999cc three-cylinder already puts out 240 hp from the factory, so most owners upgrading here are chasing one of two things: a full turbo-back exhaust for weight savings and sound with no tuning required, or an ECU tune that unlocks meaningful power gains on pump gas or race fuel through the factory electronics.

OEM Upgrade Path

There's no factory power upgrade for the Maverick R's turbo package — Can-Am's accessory catalog is limited to appearance and protection items here, which is why most performance gains on this platform come through the aftermarket instead.

Aftermarket Options

  • MBRP — Performance Series Turbo-Back: A single-exit 3-in. stainless system with dyno-verified power gains and meaningful weight savings over stock — bolts on with no tuning required.
  • RPM Powersports — Big Mouth Mid-Pipe & Q-Series Muffler: A full 3-in. mid-pipe and chambered muffler combo built to remove factory flow restrictions ahead of a turbo upgrade.
  • Treal Performance — Outlaw Turbo-Back Exhaust: A straight-through 3-in. system with dyno-tested gains on the stock tune and no tuning required.
  • EVP (Evolution Powersports) — ECU Tunes & Paragon Turbo Systems: Factory-ECU tunes scaled to fuel octane, plus a direct-replacement turbo system for owners chasing serious power beyond a bolt-on exhaust.

Maverick X3 Exhaust & Performance Upgrades

X3's turbocharged Rotax lineup already spans a wide power range across trims, so upgrades here tend to fall into the same two buckets as the Maverick R: a turbo-back exhaust for weight and sound, or an ECU flash that wakes up the factory turbo without touching hard parts.

OEM Upgrade Path

Can-Am doesn't offer a factory performance exhaust or tune for X3 — ask your dealer about accessory-catalog options for sound and appearance, but plan on the aftermarket for actual power gains.

Aftermarket Options

  • HMF Racing — Titan Series Exhaust: A common turbo-back swap for X3 owners wanting a deeper tone and modest flow gains.
  • Treal Performance — Resonated Sport Exhaust: A quieter, trail-friendly option for owners who want the turbo-back look without the loudest note.
  • Dobeck Performance — EFI Tuner: A plug-and-play tuning module that complements an exhaust upgrade without remapping the stock ECU.
  • Trinity Racing — Stage 1/2 Tunes: Dyno-developed tunes scaled to match a turbo-back exhaust and intake combination.

Defender Exhaust & Performance Upgrades

Defender's naturally aspirated Rotax engine is tuned for low-end torque and towing, not top-end horsepower — so upgrades here are less about chasing peak power and more about improving throttle response and giving the engine a deeper, more purposeful exhaust note for trail and worksite use.

OEM Upgrade Path

Ask your dealer about Can-Am's factory accessory muffler for Defender — it's a direct bolt-on that keeps emissions compliance intact, which matters if the unit will still see regular work use.

Aftermarket Options

  • HMF Racing — Utility Series Slip-On: A common upgrade for Defender owners wanting a deeper note without a full turbo-back-style system.
  • MBRP — Performance Series: A stainless slip-on aimed at improved flow and a more purposeful tone for work-and-trail use.
  • Dobeck Performance — EFI Tuner: A plug-and-play fuel tuner that smooths throttle response when paired with an aftermarket muffler.

Commander Exhaust & Performance Upgrades

Commander sits between Defender's utility focus and X3's sport tuning, and most performance upgrades here reflect that — a slip-on exhaust and light tune to sharpen throttle feel on trail, without chasing the kind of peak-power builds owners run on the turbo platforms.

OEM Upgrade Path

Check with your dealer on Can-Am's factory accessory exhaust for Commander before going aftermarket — it's a direct bolt-on that keeps the stock ECU mapping valid.

Aftermarket Options

  • HMF Racing — Titan Series Slip-On: A popular step-up for Commander owners wanting more sound and a mild flow improvement.
  • MBRP — Performance Series: A stainless slip-on option built for a more aggressive tone on trail.
  • Trinity Racing — Stage 1 Tune: A base-level tune to sharpen throttle response once an aftermarket exhaust is installed.
