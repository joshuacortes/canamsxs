Maverick R Wheels & Tires Upgrades

Maverick R and Maverick R RX come stock on 15-in. wheels with 30-in. tires, while the X RS trim steps up to 16-in. beadlock wheels wrapped in 32-in. tires. Can-Am rates the chassis for up to 35-in. tires without rubbing the tall-knuckle A-arms, so most owners upgrade to close that gap — bigger tires for more clearance and float, or true beadlock wheels for lower pressures and less risk of debeading at speed.

OEM Upgrade Path

If you have a base Maverick R or R RX, ask your dealer about stepping up to the 16-in. beadlock wheel package used on the X RS — it's a straightforward factory swap that keeps the same 6-lug pattern and bolt-on fitment.

Aftermarket Options

  • Method Race Wheels — 406 Beadlock: A common 15/16-in. beadlock swap for owners running larger tires at lower pressure.
  • Fuel Off-Road — Runner Beadlock: Lightweight forged option built for high-speed desert use.
  • Maxxis — Carnivore: A 32–35-in. tire favored for mixed desert and rocky terrain, sized to stay within Can-Am's clearance rating.
  • GBC Motorsports — Kanati Mongrel: An aggressive all-terrain tread for owners splitting time between dunes and trail.

Maverick X3 Wheels & Tires Upgrades

X3 comes stock on 14-in. wheels with 28–29-in. tires depending on trim, already sized for the platform's wide stance and long-travel suspension. Owners typically upgrade for one of two reasons: moving to a true beadlock wheel to run lower pressures on rocky or sandy terrain without debeading, or stepping up to a 30-in.-plus tire for more float and cushion at speed.

OEM Upgrade Path

Can-Am's accessory catalog offers a factory beadlock wheel option for trims that didn't come with one standard — worth checking with your dealer first if you want to stay within factory warranty terms.

Aftermarket Options

  • ITP — SS Alloy Beadlock: A lightweight, widely fitted beadlock wheel for X3 owners running desert or dune setups.
  • System 3 Off-Road — ST-3 Beadlock: Rally-proven wheel commonly paired with 30-in. tires for long-travel builds.
  • Maxxis — RAZR: A go-to all-around tire for X3 owners wanting predictable handling across mixed terrain.
  • STI — Roctane XD: A stickier compound tire aimed at rock and technical trail work.

Defender Wheels & Tires Upgrades

Defender ships with 27-in. tires on 14-in. wheels — sized for towing and payload stability rather than aggressive off-road clearance. Most upgrades here pair a mild lift with a taller, more aggressive mud or all-terrain tire, since Defender's utility geometry means tire choice has a direct effect on towing feel and load rating.

OEM Upgrade Path

Can-Am's factory accessory catalog includes a matched wheel-and-tire package sized to work with the factory 2-in. lift kit — the safest way to size up without affecting your towing rating.

Aftermarket Options

  • ITP — Coyote/Coyote Steel Wheels: A budget-friendly utility wheel commonly paired with larger mud tires.
  • Maxxis — Bighorn 2.0: A durable mud-terrain tire built for hauling and trail work.
  • GBC Motorsports — Dirt Commander: An all-terrain option for owners who want more tread life on mixed trail and farm use.

Commander Wheels & Tires Upgrades

Commander splits time between trail riding and utility work, and its stock wheel and tire package reflects that middle ground. Most owners upgrade toward a more aggressive all-terrain or mud tire for better trail grip, or a leveling kit paired with a taller tire for added ground clearance without changing the factory bolt pattern.

OEM Upgrade Path

Ask your dealer about Can-Am's factory wheel-and-tire accessory package for Commander before going aftermarket — it's a direct bolt-on that keeps everything within factory spec.

Aftermarket Options

  • Fuel Off-Road — Anza: A popular utility-style wheel for owners running larger tire fitments.
  • Maxxis — Ceros: A quieter, longer-wearing all-terrain tire suited to trail and farm use.
  • GBC Motorsports — Dirt Grip: A more aggressive tread for owners prioritizing traction over tread life.
