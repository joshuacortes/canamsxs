Maverick R Suspension Upgrades

The Maverick R already ships with heavy-duty tall-knuckle suspension and up to 25 in. of travel, so most owners upgrade for one of two reasons: adding bypass-style shocks that hold up better under sustained high-speed desert abuse, or fine-tuning compression and rebound for a specific terrain — dunes, whoops, or the rockier terrain the X rc package is built for.

OEM Upgrade Path

If your unit didn't come with Can-Am's Smart-Shox™ package from the factory, it's worth checking whether your dealer can retrofit it — Smart-Shox electronically adjusts damping in real time, which is otherwise hard to replicate with a manual aftermarket shock.

Aftermarket Options

  • FOX Racing Shox — Podium Bypass Series: External bypass tuning for riders who want adjustability without going full race-spec.
  • King Shocks — 2.5 Performance Series: Reservoir-style shocks built for repeated high-speed hits without fade.
  • Elka Suspension — Stage 5: Remote-reservoir shocks aimed at desert racing and prolonged high-load use.
  • Camburg Engineering — Long-Travel A-Arms: Paired with upgraded shocks to add travel beyond the factory geometry.

Maverick X3 Suspension Upgrades

The X3 built its reputation on class-leading stock travel and FOX Racing Shox 2.5 Podium QS3 shocks straight from the factory — already race-proven before any upgrades. Owners typically chase upgrades here for two reasons: added travel via long-travel A-arm kits for serious desert and rock racing, or bypass/reservoir shocks that handle sustained abuse better than the stock QS3 units.

OEM Upgrade Path

Trims without Smart-Shox or FOX Live Valve can often be upgraded through Can-Am's accessory catalog — worth checking with your dealer before going aftermarket if you want to keep everything under factory warranty terms.

Aftermarket Options

  • King Shocks — 2.5 Series: A common upgrade path for X3 owners who want more consistent damping at speed.
  • FOX Factory Race Series: Race-spec internals for owners pushing the X3 in competitive desert or short-course events.
  • Walker Evans Racing — Velocity Series: Needle shocks with a reputation for smooth mid-travel control.
  • S3 Power Sports — +2 Long-Travel Kits: Adds travel and width beyond the factory arms for serious off-road builds.

Defender Suspension Upgrades

Defender's suspension is tuned for towing and payload first, trail comfort second — so most upgrades here aren't about adding travel, they're about leveling the stance to clear larger tires without throwing off the geometry that makes Defender tow and haul predictably.

OEM Upgrade Path

Can-Am offers a factory 2-in. accessory lift kit through the dealer network — the safest way to gain tire clearance without affecting your factory towing rating or warranty coverage.

Aftermarket Options

  • SuperATV — Lift Kits: A common budget-friendly path to 2–3 in. of additional clearance.
  • High Lifter — Signature Series Lift Kits: Built specifically around utility SxS towing and hauling use cases.
  • FOX 2.0 IFP Shocks: A direct upgrade over stock shocks for owners who want better ride quality without a full lift.

Commander Suspension Upgrades

Commander splits the difference between trail riding and utility work, and its stock suspension reflects that — comfortable but not aggressively tuned either direction. Most upgrades here aim at either firming up handling for trail riding or leveling the stance for larger tires and added ground clearance.

OEM Upgrade Path

Ask your dealer about Can-Am's factory shock upgrade kit for Commander before going aftermarket — it's a straightforward swap that keeps everything within factory spec.

Aftermarket Options

  • Elka Suspension — Stage 4: A popular step-up for owners who split time between trail riding and utility use.
  • High Lifter — Leveling Kits: Simple, affordable clearance gains for larger tire fitment.
  • FOX 2.5 Factory Series: A higher-end option for Commander owners prioritizing ride quality on rougher trail.
