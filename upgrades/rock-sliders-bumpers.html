Maverick R Rock Sliders & Bumpers

At 77 in. wide with tall-knuckle A-arms sitting up and outside the tires, the Maverick R is wider than most sport SxS platforms and its suspension components sit more exposed than a recessed setup. Owners typically add rock sliders to protect the rocker panels on tight trails, plus a front bumper to shield the nose and mount lighting or a winch for desert running.

OEM Upgrade Path

Can-Am's accessory catalog includes a factory front bumper and skid plate package for the Maverick R — worth checking with your dealer first since it's built around the tall-knuckle geometry and won't interfere with factory sensors or the winch mount location.

Aftermarket Options

  • Assault Industries — Rival Rock Sliders: Tube-style rocker protection built to clear the Maverick R's wide stance without limiting entry/exit.
  • SuperATV — Heavy-Duty Rock Sliders: A budget-friendly bolt-on option for owners who want rocker coverage without a full armor package.
  • Dragonfire Racing — RacePace Front Bumper: A lightweight tube bumper with integrated winch mount and light bar tabs.
  • Fab Fours — Vengeance Front Bumper: A heavier-duty steel bumper for owners prioritizing max front-end protection over weight savings.

Maverick X3 Rock Sliders & Bumpers

X3's long-travel suspension and lower rocker line make it a common target for rock-strike damage on technical trail, so rock sliders are one of the most-added protection upgrades on this platform. A front bumper is the usual companion upgrade, both to protect the nose in desert running and to give owners a mounting point for a winch or auxiliary lighting.

OEM Upgrade Path

Can-Am offers a factory front bumper and rock slider package through the accessory catalog — a straightforward option if you want to stay within factory fitment and warranty terms before adding aftermarket armor.

Aftermarket Options

  • PRP Seats — Baja Rock Sliders: A common upgrade for X3 owners running technical rock trails who want direct rocker coverage.
  • S3 Power Sports — Trailing Arm Guards + Sliders: Pairs rocker protection with guards for the rear trailing arms, a frequent strike point on long-travel builds.
  • Dragonfire Racing — RacePace Front Bumper: A lightweight winch-ready bumper matched to the X3's front-end geometry.
  • Assault Industries — Vader Rear Bumper: Rear-end protection for owners who spot-turn or back into obstacles on tight trail.

Defender Rock Sliders & Bumpers

Defender's utility role means armor upgrades here are less about trail-strike protection and more about worksite durability — a front bumper that can carry a winch for recovery work, and rock sliders that double as step rails for easier cab access when loaded down with gear.

OEM Upgrade Path

Can-Am's accessory catalog includes a factory winch-ready front bumper for Defender — the safest way to add a winch mount without affecting the factory towing rating or warranty coverage.

Aftermarket Options

  • SuperATV — Rock Sliders / Nerf Bars: A popular step-rail-style slider for Defender owners who want easier cab access plus rocker protection.
  • High Lifter — Front Brush Guard: Heavy-duty brush and rock protection built for property and worksite use.
  • Fab Fours — Vengeance Front Bumper: A steel winch-ready bumper for owners doing regular recovery or towing work.

Commander Rock Sliders & Bumpers

Commander splits time between trail riding and utility work, so most owners add rock sliders for the same reason X3 owners do — protecting the rockers on technical trail — while a front bumper serves double duty as winch mount and light bar platform for after-dark utility runs.

OEM Upgrade Path

Check with your dealer about Can-Am's factory front bumper and rock slider package for Commander before going aftermarket — it's a direct bolt-on that keeps factory fitment and warranty intact.

Aftermarket Options

  • SuperATV — Rock Sliders: A budget-friendly bolt-on for basic rocker protection.
  • Dragonfire Racing — RacePace Front Bumper: A lightweight, winch-ready bumper for owners wanting recovery capability without added weight.
  • High Lifter — Signature Series Bumper: A heavier-duty option built around Commander's utility use cases.
